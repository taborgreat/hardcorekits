About

I was inspired by mc-hg, the classic McPVP server from 2012 to 2015. Can join on Java and Bedrock clients.

Pick a kit with /kit <name> before the game starts.

A minimum of 2 players to begin the three minute countdown, and up to 120 can join before it starts.

There is two minutes of invincibility before full PvP.

If you die then you are kicked until the next match.

Tip: Mushroom stew heals hearts, and your compass points at the nearest player.

A feast spawns later on for players to battle for the loot.

If a match lasts longer than 60 minutes, players are teleported to a death match arena.

Nether, Diamond, and The End are disabled, and the world is reset after each match.

Choose your kit wisely, prepare your strats, and good luck on winning.
