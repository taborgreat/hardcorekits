About

Hardcore Games for Java and Bedrock.

Pick a kit with /kit <name> before The Game starts.

A minimum of 2 players starts a 5 minute countdown, and up to 120 can join before the start.

There is two minutes of invincibility before full PvP.

If you die then you are out of The Game permanently.

Mushroom stew heals hearts, and your compass points at the nearest player.

A feast spawns later in The Game for players to battle for the loots.

If a match lasts longer than 60 minutes, players are teleported to a death match arena.

Nether, Diamond, and The End are disabled, and the world is reset after each match.



Choose your kit wisely, prepare your strats, and good luck on winning.
