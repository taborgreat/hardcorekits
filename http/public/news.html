Alpha v0.1

Back in 2012 to 2015 I fell in love with a very unique Minecraft server. Rushing home from middle school, I would hop into a new game and try to get another win.

This was pre Fortnite. A popular book series called The Hunger Games came out around 2010 and most kids were reading it. The idea of being thrown into an arena where everyone had to fight to the death and the last one standing was the victor was huge.

Most Minecraft Hunger Games servers were generic prebuilt maps with chests to find, or highly overdone kits, and so on. But this server generated a completely natural world and let players choose from a handful of well balanced kits to dig, mine, build, set traps, and use everything Minecraft has to offer along with the kits to create a gaming rush unlike anything I have found since.

The servers shut down, and while McPVP has recently been revived (same name, different brand) and the Hardcore Games mode has been revived several times, none of them matched what I remembered.

I have carefully crafted an experience matching what most of us who played in 2013 would genuinely expect. Straightforward and fun, along with a collection of kits.

Not many people play yet. In fact, as I write this, no one does. But as more people play and test things and report bugs, we will get more servers opened up and start developing new kits if the community wants. I am very open to the community for suggestions, support, and help.

So as a baseline, announcing a solid and working rebuild of the experience I remember: Alpha v0.1 of Hardcore Games is released. These are the kits in the first version.

  • Anchor: no knockback given or taken, no boots
  • Barbarian: a sword that upgrades itself as you get kills
  • Beastmaster: tamed wolves with speed or regeneration
  • Berserker: kills give Strength, but no jumping while it lasts
  • Cannibal: hitting players feeds you and starves them
  • Cookiemonster: grass drops cookies that heal and speed you up
  • Cultivator: everything you plant grows instantly
  • Demoman: hidden pressure plate mines
  • Digger: dragon eggs that open a shaft under whoever stands there
  • Endermage: a portal that drags anyone above or below it to you
  • Fighter: a wooden sword and two steak, nothing else
  • Fireman: immune to fire, lava and lightning
  • Fisherman: a rod that reels players to your feet
  • Flash: teleport where you look, at a cost
  • Forger: smelt ore in your inventory with coal
  • Gambler: a button with random effects, good or very bad
  • Gladiator: force a 1v1 in a sky arena
  • Grandpa: a stick with Knockback II
  • Grappler: a hook that launches you toward whatever it catches
  • Hades: turn mobs into minions that fight for you
  • Hermit: start alone, far from everyone
  • Hulk: pick players up and throw them
  • Jackhammer: a hammer that brings down whole columns
  • Jellyfish: water wherever you click, no bucket
  • Kangaroo: a rocket that launches you through the air
  • Kaya: grass that gives way under enemies
  • Launcher: sponges that fling whoever steps on them
  • Monk: knock the item out of an enemy's hand
  • Ninja: teleport to the last player you hit
  • Poseidon: hit harder in water, never drown
  • Pyro: fire charges that set the area ablaze
  • Reaper: a hoe that withers players
  • Scorch: leave a trail of fire behind you
  • Snail: hits can slow enemies down
  • Soulstealer: one chance to come back from death
  • Spiderman: snowballs that turn into cobwebs
  • Spy: a compass that tells you far too much
  • Stomper: land on people and they take your fall damage
  • Switcher: snowballs that swap places with whoever they hit
  • Tank: players you kill explode, explosions never hurt you
  • Thor: call lightning down with your axe
  • Timelord: freeze everyone around you for ten seconds
  • Turtle: crouch to take almost no damage
  • Vampire: kills heal you, potions work backwards
  • Viper: hits can poison
  • Werewolf: strong at night, weak by day, wolves leave you alone
  • Wisp: decoys of yourself that scatter

Thank you all for being here, and I invite you to share this around and get your friends. We need an organic, real community and the only way that can happen is by word of mouth. I genuinely think the experience becomes much greater with 40 or more players per game, and 120 is peak.

Welcome to Hardcore Games with kits.
